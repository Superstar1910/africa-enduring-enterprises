# Rebuilding Kakira: capital structure of the 1988 sugar rehabilitation proposal

*Research note | 19 October 2026 | Contemporary appraisal estimates, not verified disbursements*

The [World Bank's 7 March 1988 staff appraisal report](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/546331468175742480/pdf/multi-page.pdf) describes a proposed **US$61.5 million** sugar rehabilitation programme in Uganda. Its financing table allocates **US$24.9m new IDA**, **US$7.4m existing IDA**, **US$8.7m AfDB**, **US$9.4m AfDF** and **US$11.1m KSW**. The five amounts sum to US$61.5m. These amounts are planned sources in the appraisal, **not verified actual commitments or disbursements**.

The appraisal lists a US$24.6m on-lending component to KSW at **8.75% interest**, repayable over **15 years**, including a **five-year moratorium** on principal and interest; KSW would bear foreign-exchange risk. The original IDA credit was proposed at US$24.9m equivalent, with a 40-year maturity to the sovereign borrower. Distinguish sovereign concessional terms from the project's on-lending terms.

The report projected restoring sugar production from zero to an annual historical level of 85,000 tonnes. It estimated project foreign-exchange savings and employment benefits; these are **forecasts**, not realised outcomes.

## Research significance

This episode suggests that long-lived family business lineage and industrial recovery can depend on multiple institutions: the family, the state, development lenders and management capability. The appraisal also identifies unresolved ownership interests and government–minority-shareholder disputes as risks.

## Follow-up evidence required

1. IDA credit approval and completion report to establish actual disbursement and implementation outcomes.
2. AfDB/AfDF completion records and actual lending terms.
3. Audited KSW accounts before and after rehabilitation.
4. 1985 joint venture agreement and original incorporation certificate.
5. Production and capacity-utilisation time series.
6. Foreign-exchange exposure, debt service and refinancing history.

The corresponding `data/financing-events.csv` records **planned** financing only. It does not record these as audited financial results.
