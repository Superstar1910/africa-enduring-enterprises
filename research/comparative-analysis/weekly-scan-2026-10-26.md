# Africa's Enduring Enterprises — evidence update, 26 October 2026

**Scope:** founding dates, ownership transitions, executive succession, anniversaries and implications for institutional longevity. This is an evidence log, not a ranking of Africa's oldest surviving legal entities.

## Newly strengthened evidence

### New Kenya Co-operative Creameries (Kenya) — a centenarian lineage, not a centenarian company
- **1925**: original Kenya Co-operative Creameries founding year reported in the *Kenya Yearbook 2019*; **secondary government history**. [Source](https://fliphtml5.com/yvqhd/dkpl/YEARBOOK_2019/432/).
- **24 June 2003**: New Kenya Co-operative Creameries registered under the Co-operative Societies Act to take over KCC 2000 Limited's assets/business/control/management; **documented in the entity's official annual financial statements**.
- **19 November 2004**: New KCC incorporated under the Companies Act with **100% Government of Kenya shareholding**, taking over from the 2003 cooperative society; **documented in official financial statements**. [Treasury-hosted financial statements](https://www.treasury.go.ke/sites/default/files/financial%20statements/New-Kenya-Co-operative-Creameries-Limited.pdf).
- **Interpretation:** a 101-year *institutional and brand lineage* in 2026, but the current state-owned company was incorporated in 2004. No claim of uninterrupted cooperative ownership or identical legal identity is warranted.
- **Next evidence:** original 1925 cooperative registration, 2000 company transfer documents, 2003 registration certificate, 2004 incorporation certificate, and current government shareholder register.

### Nigerian Breweries (Nigeria) — 80 years, ownership concentration and executive succession
- **1946**: Nigerian Brewery Limited incorporated following arrangements between UAC and HEINEKEN, per the [official 80th anniversary history](https://80.nbplc.com/); **official company claim**. Precise 1946 day/month remains unverified from the original certificate. This is **not** evidence of indigenous Nigerian founding.
- **5 September 1973**: share trading admission date, per [investor relations](https://www.nbplc.com/investor-relations/).
- **30 September 2020**: HEINEKEN shareholding **56.13%**; **31 December 2025**: **72.9%** (an increase of **16.77 percentage points**). Both figures are company disclosures on the same investor-relations page. The cause and dates of intervening transfers/issuances remain to be independently reconciled.
- **15 May 2025**: [exchange-filed company notification](https://doclib.ngxgroup.com/Financial_NewsDocs/44113_NIGERIAN_BREWERIES_PLC.-NOTIFICATION_OF_BOARD_CHANGES_CORPORATE_ACTIONS_MAY_2025.pdf) confirms Hans Essaadi's term ended **30 June 2025** and Thibaut Boidin succeeded him **1 July 2025**. The company also describes a **NGN 550 billion rights issue**; net proceeds and dilution mechanics need verification from issue documents and filings.
- **September 2026**: [80th anniversary campaign](https://www.nbplc.com/nigerian-breweries-launches-campaign-to-mark-80th-anniversary/) publicised, with independent contemporary reporting dated **21 September 2026**. [Contemporary coverage](https://nairametrics.com/2026/09/21/nigerian-breweries-flags-off-80th-anniversary-celebration-with-nationwide-search-for-shared-memories/).
- **Interpretation:** longevity across executive turnover and an evolving international shareholder base is a useful *hypothesis* about institutional continuity, not proof that the governance model caused survival.

### Ethiopian Airlines (Ethiopia) — 80 years of operations
- Official overview distinguishes **21 December 1945 founding** from **8 April 1946 start of operations**, with **100% Ethiopian government ownership**. [Official overview](https://corporate.ethiopianairlines.com/aboutethiopian/overview).
- The airline [commemorated 80 years of operations on 8 April 2026](https://corporate.ethiopianairlines.com/Press-release-open-page/eight-decades-of-soaring-high-ethiopian-airlines-marks-80-years-of-service).
- **Interpretation:** date precision matters; do not describe 2026 as the 80th anniversary of legal founding. Continuity of leadership pipelines remains a testable question, not an established causal explanation.

## Selected feature outline — Nigerian Breweries

**Working headline:** *Who Owns an 80-Year-Old Company? Nigerian Breweries and the Institutions Beyond Its Founders*

**Central proposition:** Eighty years of survival do not answer who founded a company, who controls it now, or how governance and access to capital enable it to outlive successive leaders.

1. **The anniversary and its paradox.** Incorporated in 1946; 2026 anniversary; original UAC–HEINEKEN arrangement. Explain why a Nigerian company is not automatically an indigenous-founded company. [Official anniversary](https://80.nbplc.com/).
2. **From brewery to listed institution.** First locally brewed Star lager in June 1949; listed 5 September 1973; mergers and portfolio evolution. Distinguish incorporation, operations and listing. [Company history](https://www.nbplc.com/our-company/), [IR](https://www.nbplc.com/investor-relations/).
3. **Ownership as an evolving compact.** HEINEKEN 56.13% in September 2020 versus 72.9% in December 2025; public shareholders and local capital-market access. Examine rights issue and ownership dilution only after obtaining full allotment data. [IR](https://www.nbplc.com/investor-relations/).
4. **Management beyond the founding generation.** Hans Essaadi to Thibaut Boidin, 1 July 2025; board-approved transition while company recapitalised. [NGX filing](https://doclib.ngxgroup.com/Financial_NewsDocs/44113_NIGERIAN_BREWERIES_PLC.-NOTIFICATION_OF_BOARD_CHANGES_CORPORATE_ACTIONS_MAY_2025.pdf).
5. **Institutional lesson.** Longevity may reflect repeatable governance, external capital, management succession and brand distribution—but these are mechanisms to investigate, not causal findings. Compare with indigenous-founded firms and cooperatives, not just other multinationals.
6. **Evidence gaps / research questions.** Original incorporation certificate; complete 2024 rights issue terms and allotments; ownership register before and after the issue; board succession and performance data; historical distress/restructuring episodes.

**Editorial status:** sourced outline / not a completed forensic case study. **Distribution:** publish the research note on legesi.xyz first; adapt a shorter Substack edition only after canonical page is live.
