# Contributing research

Open an issue proposing an enterprise or correction. Include the legal name, country, origin basis, original document or archival link, and any continuity caveat. Do not change a record to `verified` without primary evidence and independent review. Use ISO 8601 dates for events; use empty cells for unknown values, never invented zeroes. Validate with `python3 scripts/validate.py` and rebuild with `python3 scripts/generate_site.py`.
