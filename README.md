# Africa's Enduring Enterprises

**The companies that survived. The institutions they built. The lessons for Africa's next century.**

An open research and editorial project by [legesi.xyz](https://legesi.xyz), with features intended for [legesi.substack.com](https://legesi.substack.com).

## Research scope

Century-old businesses, emerging centenarians, cooperatives, disrupted enterprises and future institutions. We distinguish company incorporation from predecessor lineages, family enterprise origins, brand survival, and interrupted operations. The initial register is **provisional and not a verified ranking**.

## Repository
- `data/candidates.csv`: candidate register and evidence status.
- `methodology/verification.md`: research and publication standards.
- `profiles/`: editorial research briefs.
- `site/index.html`: public research directory prototype.
- `.github/workflows/validate.yml`: automated register validation.

## Editorial governance
No unsupported claim of continuous operations or indigenous founding. Historical dates must be linked to primary sources before being classified as verified. Establishment-age distributions are not survival probabilities.
