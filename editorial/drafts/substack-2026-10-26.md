## Who really owns an 80-year-old African company?

The more interesting question about an enduring African enterprise is not simply when it began, but which institutions survived its founders.

Nigerian Breweries traces its incorporation to 1946 through UAC and HEINEKEN. Its 2026 anniversary is an occasion to examine governance rather than celebrate age alone. Company disclosures report that HEINEKEN owned 56.13% in September 2020 and 72.9% by December 2025. A 2025 exchange notification records the transition from Hans Essaadi to Thibaut Boidin and describes a major rights issue. The shareholding changes still require transaction-level reconciliation.

Compare Kenya Co-operative Creameries: its lineage dates to 1925, but official financial statements identify a new cooperative in 2003 and a distinct government-owned company in 2004. Ethiopian Airlines likewise distinguishes founding in December 1945 from operations beginning in April 1946.

The point is not to rank birthdays. It is to ask what persists through changes in ownership, leadership, legal form and access to capital.
