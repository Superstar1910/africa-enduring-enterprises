## Editorial governance

1. Publish original research and full editorial articles on legesi.xyz first.
2. Confirm successful Pages deployment and HTTP availability before distributing links.
3. Link claims to the source register; label official self-reported histories, independently verified records, projections and hypotheses separately.
4. Distinguish company incorporation, predecessor lineage, asset continuity and family-group continuity.
5. Distribute selectively. LinkedIn versions should be self-contained, shorter, and link to the canonical research. Substack may carry selected full features with a prominent canonical link and clear attribution.
6. Record distribution URL, date, channel, version and any corrections. Never mark as distributed until confirmed.
7. Update RSS/feed, writing sitemap and archive indexing before considering the native publication pipeline complete.
