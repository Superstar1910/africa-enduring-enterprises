THE CONVERSATION THAT STARTED THE RESEARCH

From a question about Uganda's oldest businesses to a research programme

“Entrepreneurs start the businesses but never build the governance and institutions to ensure their longevity beyond the founder's vision.”

— Kenneth Legesi, responding to the original X post by @ekiggundu.

The response was accompanied by a handwritten Uganda Business Longevity & Institution-Building Map, bringing together enterprise-age data, examples of longstanding Ugandan businesses, the succession challenge, and the institutional reforms needed to build enduring enterprises.

Read the original conversation on X ↗
