# Research framework

## Unit of observation
A record may describe a legal entity, brand, group, predecessor lineage or cooperative. Never equate these without documentary proof. Track each ownership or succession event separately.

## Research questions
1. When and by whom was the institution founded? 2. What persisted: the company, its brand, its assets or its predecessor lineage? 3. How did ownership and leadership change? 4. What evidence supports financial and operational continuity? 5. What transferable governance and capital-allocation lessons follow?

## Classification
African-incorporated, African-founded, indigenous African-founded, immigrant-founded and contemporary African-owned are different attributes. Ownership is time-varying.

## Research maturity
Candidate -> source collection -> independently corroborated -> reviewed -> published -> periodically refreshed. A company may remain provisional indefinitely.
