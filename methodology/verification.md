# Verification protocol

1. Distinguish **legal entity**, **brand**, **predecessor lineage**, **founder commercial activity**, and **cooperative**.
2. Capture exact dates with documentary evidence: registration filings, annual reports, official archives, historical newspapers and independent scholarship.
3. Record founder nationality/identity separately from contemporary ownership and country of incorporation. Do not assume Africa-incorporated means indigenous-founded.
4. Track mergers, nationalisation, insolvency, reconstitution, interruption and succession as dated events.
5. Use evidence statuses `verified`, `partially_verified`, `disputed`, `unverified`. No unverified candidate may be described as definitively oldest.
6. Separate establishment age distribution from survival rates; do not infer causal relationships from cross-sectional counts.
7. Review source links and data annually; record editor, retrieval date, and corrections.
8. Keep images and logos subject to rights clearance. Publish analytical assessments with explicit uncertainty.
