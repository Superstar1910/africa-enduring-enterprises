# Editorial and corrections policy

Every material historical or financial claim must have a source ID and a traceable reference. Prefer original registration documents, annual reports, archival records and audited accounts; triangulate company claims with independent sources. Distinguish facts, management claims and analysis. Record dates of access, author and correction rationale. Avoid unsupported superlatives and retrospective certainty. Disclose interruptions, restructurings and ownership changes. Obtain image rights. No investment recommendation is implied.
