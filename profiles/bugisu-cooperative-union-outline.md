# Before Independence, There Was Bugisu Coffee

**Provisional feature outline — research in progress**

## Thesis
A Ugandan farmer-owned cooperative whose institutional roots predate independence provides a test case for collective capital formation, governance and recovery.

## Research questions
- What documentary records establish the July 1954 union origin and predecessor cooperatives?
- How were member ownership, board elections and coffee sales governed?
- Which political and market disruptions materially affected operations?
- Did reported 2025 coffee sales revenue of UGX 19bn versus UGX 6bn in 2024 reflect prices, volumes or accounting changes?
- Can processing investment improve member returns without weakening financial resilience?

## Evidence needed
Original cooperative registration; audited financial statements 2023–2025; AGM minutes; primary society register; coffee volumes; government records; interviews with management and members.

**Editorial caution:** Revenue figures are reported claims, not independently audited in this project. A long corporate history does not prove uninterrupted operating continuity or profitability.
