# The Mulwana Industrial Legacy

**Feature brief — provisional**

Examine Uganda Batteries and Nice House of Plastics as case studies of founder-led industrialisation and the transition to successor leadership. Test founding dates independently: the Uganda Batteries precursor lineage is disputed (1961 versus 1967). Separate the founding of each legal entity from the business group's overall history.

Research: incorporation records, interviews, historical annual reports, changes in shareholding, professional management, reinvestment, industrial diversification and succession after James Mulwana.

Central question: What governance and capital allocation choices allowed an indigenous Ugandan industrial group to outlive its founder?
